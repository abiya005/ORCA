Threat Intelligence Database

CBI & Cybercrime Alerts Linked
DIGITAL ARREST

Mumbai Police/CBI Video Detention

Scammers contact citizens claiming their Aadhaar card is linked to money laundering or drug parcels. They demand they stay on a video call (Skype/WhatsApp) indefinitely to execute a "digital arrest."

Indicators: "Digital arrest", Skype verification, "don't contact family", parcel seizure.
FAKE HR

Telegram Task / YouTube Likes Scam

HR agents offer work-from-home tasks, such as liking YouTube videos or review rating. After giving a small commission (Rs 150-500), they coerce the victim to join VIP groups and invest money in fake crypto websites.

Indicators: "WhatsApp task agent", Google Maps rating, payment screenshots, VIP channel.
APK MALWARE

Fake Utility App (SMS Forwarder)

Scammers send APK packages claiming to be updates for banking, electricity bills, or support. Once installed, it requests SMS permissions to silently read bank OTPs and forward them to the attacker's server.

Permissions: RECEIVE_SMS, SEND_SMS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED.
