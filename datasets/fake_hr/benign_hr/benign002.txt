Dear Candidates,

The Placement Office is pleased to announce the upcoming campus recruitment drive for Google India Development Center for the role of Software Development Engineer (SDE-1).

Eligibility Criteria:
- B.Tech / M.Tech / Dual Degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or related discipline.
- Graduation Year: 2026.
- Cumulative Performance Index (CPI): 7.0 and above.

Interview Process:
1. Online Coding Assessment (2 Problem statements on Data Structures & Algorithms).
2. Technical Interview Round 1 (System Design & Data Structures).
3. Technical Interview Round 2 (Algorithms & Problem Solving).
4. HR Interaction Round.

Important Note:
All registration must be completed strictly through the official IIT Bombay Placement Cell Portal at placement.iitb.ac.in. No registration fee or payment is required at any stage of the recruitment process.
