TITLE:
Public Advisory on Digital Arrest Frauds – Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C)

SOURCE:
Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India <advisory@cybercrime.gov.in>

DATE:
Fri, May 15, 11:00 AM

ARTICLE:

PUBLIC SAFETY ADVISORY

The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) and Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) issue this public notice regarding fraudulent calls impersonating law enforcement officers.

Key Guidelines for Citizens:
1. No Police, CBI, Enforcement Directorate (ED), RBI, or Customs official conducts legal interrogations or arrests via Skype, WhatsApp, or Zoom video calls.
2. The concept of "Digital Arrest" does not exist under Indian law.
3. Law enforcement agencies NEVER ask citizens to transfer money to "safe accounts", "verification accounts", or RBI escrow funds.
4. If you receive a suspicious call threatening arrest or money laundering charges, stay calm, disconnect immediately, and do not transfer any funds.

Reporting Channels:
- Call National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930
- Register Complaint: cybercrime.gov.in
- Visit nearest local Police Station.

Issued in public interest by Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.
